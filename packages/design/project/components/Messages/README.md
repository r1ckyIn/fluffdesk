What the customer sent and what the agent answered, as a quiet transcript.

- `.msg.msg-customer` on the left in `raised`; `.msg.msg-agent` on the right on `sheet` with a 1px `border`. Neither bubble is filled with `accent`: the transcript is evidence, not an action.
- `.msg-tool` is a one-line `small` `muted` note, right-aligned, saying what the agent looked up ("Agent looked up quote: AUD 90–120"); an error outcome turns it `declined`.
- Customer photos sit inside the customer bubble as 96px squares, radius 8. A removed photo shows a `raised` box with "Photo removed after 24 hours".
- Max width 78% of the column (92% on the phone), radius 12 with a 4px inner corner, text in `body` with `pre-wrap`.
